HOW THE TIMELINE WORKS

Your Marvel marathon, mapped across the multiverse.

This timeline is primarily arranged chronologically according to when the events of each project take place. Where a project spans multiple years, placement is based on the period in which the majority of the story occurs, with significant flashbacks, flash forwards and other time periods noted separately.

Projects from different universes are included where they have meaningful connections to the wider Marvel multiverse. Where chronology cannot be reliably established, projects are placed according to release order or narrative relevance.
